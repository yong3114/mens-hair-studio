Expected transition examples
----------------------------
New lead:
  Stage=New, Interest=Unknown, Follow-up=null

First WhatsApp, customer asks price:
  Contact outcome=Interested
  Stage: New -> Contacted
  Interest: Interested
  Follow-up remains null unless explicitly chosen

Customer says "I will think about it next week":
  Contact outcome=Considering
  Stage remains Contacted
  Interest=Considering
  Follow-up=chosen date

Consultation booked:
  Stage=Consultation booked
  Interest becomes Interested only if it was still Unknown

Consultation completed but customer needs time:
  Stage=Consultation done
  Interest=Considering
  Follow-up=chosen date

Signed:
  Stage=Signed
  Interest=Interested
  Follow-up cleared

Not signed / closed:
  Stage=Lost
  Interest=Not interested
  Follow-up cleared
